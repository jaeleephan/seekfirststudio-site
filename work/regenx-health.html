The brief

REGENX Health wanted an about film that introduces Dr. Marshall Stein and the practice, told by the people who know it best.

The approach

We started with a written treatment and shot plan, so everyone agreed on the story before a camera came out.

On the shoot, we interviewed Dr. Stein in a clean, lit setup and filmed athletes training, then cut the film around their own words.

What we made

A 2 minute 26 second about film for REGENX Health.
