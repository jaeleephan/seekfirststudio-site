Paris Moran

Why we made it

We make original films about people worth following. We've known Paris since 2020, and his fight career was a story we wanted to tell on our own terms.

The approach

We scouted the gym ahead of time to plan around its window light. Then we filmed Paris training, wrapping his hands and working in the ring.

We kept the teaser in black and white so nothing pulls focus from him.

What we made

A 1 minute 9 second film, a black and white teaser, and a set of stills for social.
